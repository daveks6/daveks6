# Privacy Policy

**JARVIS Companion — Volvo Integration**  
**Effective date: 6 November 2026**

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the personal, non-commercial JARVIS Companion Volvo integration handles information when a user chooses to connect a Volvo account.

JARVIS Companion is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Volvo Cars.

## 2. Information handled

Depending on the services and permissions made available by Volvo Cars, the integration may handle:

- Volvo authorisation tokens and related connection information;
- vehicle identity and connectivity status;
- charging, battery, or fuel information;
- odometer or distance information; and
- the vehicle's last reported location or related status.

The exact information available depends on the vehicle, account, supported APIs, and permissions granted by the user.

## 3. Purpose and legal basis

Information is handled only to authenticate the user's requested connection and display supported read-only vehicle information within JARVIS Companion. The integration does not use Volvo data for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated commercial purposes.

Processing is initiated at the user's request and is necessary to provide the personally configured integration. Where consent is the applicable basis, the user may withdraw it by disconnecting the integration or revoking access through the relevant Volvo account controls.

## 4. Storage and security

Authorisation credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally on the user's device using the application's encrypted storage. Vehicle information is processed locally for display and operation of the requested features.

No credentials or access tokens are intentionally published in this repository or embedded in these public legal pages. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and accounts.

## 5. Sharing

JARVIS Companion does not sell personal information or vehicle data. Information is exchanged with Volvo Cars only as necessary to authenticate and retrieve the data requested by the user. Information may also be processed by the user's device and operating-system components required to run the application.

Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights, security, or safety.

## 6. Retention

Locally stored connection information is retained while the integration remains connected or until it is removed by the user, the application is reset or uninstalled, or access is revoked. Temporary data may be kept only as needed for operation, troubleshooting, and security.

## 7. User choices and rights

Users can stop future access by disconnecting the Volvo integration, removing locally stored connection details, uninstalling the application, or revoking the application's access through Volvo account controls.

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing of their personal information. Requests concerning information controlled directly by Volvo Cars should be made to Volvo Cars through its official privacy channels.

## 8. International services

Volvo Cars and relevant platform services may process information in countries other than the user's country. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy notices and terms.

## 9. Children's privacy

The integration is not directed to children and is intended only for an authorised Volvo account holder or vehicle user.

## 10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the integration, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

## 11. Contact

Privacy questions about JARVIS Companion can be raised through the contact details associated with the application record in the Volvo Cars Developer Portal.
